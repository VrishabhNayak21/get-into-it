A hackathon is an intense, time-bound event where developers, designers, and innovators collaborate to solve real-world problems through coding and creativity. Participants form teams, brainstorm ideas, and build functional prototypes within 24-hours. It's a great opportunity to learn, network, and showcase skills while competing for prizes and recognition.

PRE-REQUISITES

  • Basic understanding of programming, problem-solving, and teamwork.
  • Bring your own laptop, chargers, and necessary software or tools pre-installed.
  • Participants should stay on-site for 24 hours.

Join us for an unforgettable experience of innovation, collaboration, and coding brilliance. Build solutions, make connections, and compete for glory!
